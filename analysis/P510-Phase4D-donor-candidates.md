## Complete camera versus standalone board

| Consideration | Broken complete P510 | Standalone P510 motherboard |
|---|---|---|
| Model identity | Can be checked against exterior labels and, if already available, owner-visible menus; seller claims still need verification | Depends heavily on seller labeling and board markings that are currently unknown |
| Firmware-bearing components | Board and storage remain together if intact | Board is present, but exact revision/storage state can be difficult to establish |
| Board condition | Lens/LCD/control faults may leave electronics intact; water, corrosion, or electrical damage can affect everything | Seller can misidentify or substitute a different model/revision; no camera-level functional test |
| Physical access | Requires disassembly and therefore trained repair work; not the working camera | Easier to inspect both board faces if the seller supplies clear images |
| Ability to test | May allow non-invasive power/function checks only under a separately approved plan | Usually cannot be tested without associated parts or a controlled fixture |
| Current value | Better future evidence if the fault is clearly non-board-related and the mainboard is intact | Useful for photography and markings only if model/revision are independently confirmed |

If future evidence establishes a storage target and a safe read-only plan, a **complete parts P510 with an intact mainboard and a non-board fault** is preferable to a bare board for model/revision verification and context. A standalone board may be preferable only after its model and revision are verified and it can be documented from both sides. Neither option is justified for purchase at this stage.

## Donor criteria for a future decision

Prefer a candidate described and photographed as:

- Nikon COOLPIX P510, with clear exterior model markings;
- lens error/stuck lens, broken LCD, damaged controls, or cosmetic damage rather than a board-level fault;
- intact main PCB, visible memory/major ICs, and no missing or harvested components;
- no liquid exposure, corrosion, burn marks, or evidence of electrical surge;
- both board faces photographed sharply before purchase, with sticker and any revision markings readable;
- seller willing to confirm that the board is present and not previously removed or substituted.

Avoid water damage, heavy corrosion, electrical/lightning damage, missing/harvested boards, and listings with no useful internal photos. A cheap camera with a destroyed mainboard is a poor donor.

## Next documentation requests (not sent)

Before any purchase, ask for photographs of both sides at original resolution, a close-up of every large IC marking with thermal pads/shields already absent only if the seller has already removed them, the board sticker/assembly ID, and confirmation that the mainboard is intact. Do not ask a seller to remove shields or components solely for this project; do not authorize any physical work on the owner's working P510.
