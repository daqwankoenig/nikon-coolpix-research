Do not name or contact an independent repair business based on non-Nikon listings. Ask Nikon Technical Support or Service and Repair Support for a current official referral if needed.
- **Nikon factory service:** Nikon's official Product Repair page confirms Nikon factory mail-in and in-person service for nearly all Nikon products. That is the verified service route; it does not establish that the discontinued P510 is currently accepted or that firmware reflash media remains available.
